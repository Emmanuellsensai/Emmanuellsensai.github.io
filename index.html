Kola, AI Voice Agent (KOLAPPTECH LTD)

A multilingual voice agent that calls customers back in English, Nigerian Pidgin or Yoruba for banks and businesses: payment reminders, order and support follow-ups, and re-engagement. Built with FastAPI, Next.js, ElevenLabs, Africa's Talking and Twilio, with a live operator dashboard, transcripts and consent-first call rules.
