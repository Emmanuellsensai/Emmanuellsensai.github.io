Projects

Kola for Banks (KOLAPPTECH LTD)

An AI voice agent for bank customer service. It plugs into the bank's own API, calls customers back in English, Nigerian Pidgin or Yoruba, and helps with balances, virtual accounts and onboarding invites. Live with BMONI as the reference integration, with an operator dashboard, call transcripts and consent-first call rules.

  • Visit Live Site

Kola for Business

Kola for Nigerian businesses: it confirms orders, updates customers when a delivery is late, chases unpaid payments with a payment link, and asks how it went, in English, Pidgin, Yoruba, Igbo or Hausa. Built with FastAPI, Next.js, ElevenLabs, Africa's Talking and Twilio.

  • Visit Live Site

Toolzng, Academic Formatter

A live automation tool built for Nigerian students in Go and vanilla JS. It handles APA 7th Edition formatting, verifies DOIs against Crossref using concurrent goroutines, and generates Preliminary Pages instantly.
